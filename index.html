Uganda

Uganda is a landlocked country in East Africa known for it's diverse landscape beauty with snow-capped Rwenzori Mountains and immense Lake Victoria. Its abundant wildlife includes chimpanzees as well as rare birds and mountain gorillas.Murchison Falls National Park in the northwest is known for its 43m-tall waterfall and wildlife such as hippos. The people of Uganda make it the best it can ever be, with various tribes and over 50 languages spoken, its really a blessed country
